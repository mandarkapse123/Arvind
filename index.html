Arvind & Mandar

Birthday Special Edition • Forever & Beyond

Prologue • The Colony Days

Two Decades in the Making

It began nearly twenty years ago in the familiar lanes of our colony.

Back then, we were just two school kids with boundless energy, wild imaginations, and zero common sense. Separately, we were ordinary boys trying to survive homework and school bells.

But the day our paths crossed, the universe set into motion a brotherhood that no amount of time, distance, or growing up could ever shake.

📸 The Colony Days • Where It All Began
1
Chapter I • The Sound of Freedom

Screaming Into The Wind

There was nothing quite like riding that bike together through the colony streets. We had an unwritten tradition: inventing totally nonsensical, made-up songs and belting them out as loudly and out-of-tune as humanly possible, straight from the bottom of our throats.

Pedestrians stared. Dogs barked. We didn't give a damn. The worse we sang, the harder we laughed — until our stomachs ached and tears streamed down our faces.

"We weren't singers. We were setting the world's most chaotic soundtrack to the best years of our lives."
2
Chapter II • The Stunt Arena

Ground Zero Legends

Every superhero duo needs a testing ground. Ours had a name: Ground Zero.

That open patch of ground was our personal stunt arena. The goal? Master the legendary 'one-wheeler' bike stunt.

The reality? Spectacular, catastrophic, unadulterated failure. Every single attempt ended with us nearly flying off, wobbling uncontrollably, and cheering each other on like we had just won an Olympic gold.

🏍️ Ground Zero Stunt Arena
3
Chapter II — Continued

Act Casual, We're Out of Petrol

And then came the inevitable moment every ride: the engine would sputter and die. Out of petrol. In the middle of nowhere.

Did we panic? Never. We deployed the ultimate technique: one leg stuck out, paddling and stoking the bike along the pavement, looking cool and calm.

We maintained direct eye contact with everyone passing by, pretending that gliding a dead bike with one leg was 100% intentional and peak style.

⛽

"Rule #1: Even when the tank is empty, the swagger is forever full."

4
Chapter III • Time Distortion

The Legend of 'Arvind's 2 Minutes'

Arvind had a superpower that baffled modern science: his relationship with time.

Whenever he said, "Amma, I'll be back in 2 min," every clock in the neighborhood immediately surrendered. That '2 minutes' had an uncanny way of morphing into 2 hours, and on truly legendary days, a full 8-hour adventure.

Chai stops, endless colony debates, and unplanned detours — those '2 minutes' were always the best hours of the week.

⏰ 2 Minutes = 8 Hours of Shenanigans
5
Chapter IV • The Colony Arena

Trump Cards & Loyal Companions

Long afternoons were settled over intense Trump Card battles. Speed, power, cylinders — we knew every stat by heart.

And then there were our juniors. Each of us had that one favorite junior we would playfully tease and boss around — a special bond where they loved being part of our circle just as much as we loved having them around.

It was our little kingdom, and we ruled it with laughter.

"In our colony, cards were currency, and our circle was unbreakable."
6
Chapter V • The Unspoken Archives

A Thousand Unwritten Stories

Twenty years cannot fit into ten pages. And to be honest, a couple thousand of our stories can never be shared in public anyway.

Allegedly, we found something and buried it by digging a hole... those graves should never be dug up again!

The late-night conspiracies, the secret escapes, the moments of pure unadulterated madness — those remain securely locked in the vault between us. Not every memory needs words; each one holds a permanent, sacred place in our hearts.

🔒 The Brotherhood Vault • Top Secret
7
Chapter VI • The Ultimate Power

Without A Care In The World

People spend their entire lives searching for a superpower. Despite the fact that we all secretly dreamed of saving the school from a terrorist attack, we discovered we had some other superpower twenty years ago: when we are together, the world simply fades away.

No deadlines, no anxieties, no burdens of adult life. In each other's company, we step right back into that carefree bubble where anything is possible and nothing can touch us.

🌟

"Together, we don't just survive the chaos — we make the world bend to our laughter."

8
The 20-Year Promise

Happy Birthday, Arvind! 🎂

In this world, life happens. Everyone builds their path, takes on responsibilities, and moves across time and space as they should.

Yet here is our truth: whenever we meet — whether after three days or three years — we will always hold that exact same superpower. In the midst of all the world's chaos, we will forever have the power to live without a single care in the world.

🎉 Happy Birthday, Russian Brother! 🎉

To 20 glorious years behind us, and a lifetime of world-bending ahead.

— Always by your side, Mandar
